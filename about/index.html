About Marks and Grades

Marks and Grades is a set of free calculators for students: percentage of marks, CGPA to percentage, SGPA and CGPA from credits, the marks needed to reach a target, and attendance. Beside the calculators it explains, in plain words, what the rules of some boards and universities actually say.

Where the rules come from

Every board or university rule on this site is taken from that body's own document. We read these on 12 November 2026:

  • CBSE Circular No. 24 of 28 May 2010: grading at secondary level, CGPA and the indicative percentage (9.5 × CGPA)
  • CBSE Frequently Asked Questions, 2016: how CGPA is calculated, 75% attendance, pass rules
  • CBSE Examination Bye-laws, Scheme of Examinations and Pass Criteria (copy in the bye-laws archive on cbse.gov.in)
  • CBSE: Grading System in CBSE for 10th and 12th (listed on cbse.gov.in with the date 08/07/2020)
  • CISCE: ICSE Year 2028, Regulations for Examination (January 2026)
  • CISCE: ISC Year 2028, Regulations for Indian School Certificate Examination (January 2026)
  • University Grants Commission: Guidelines on Adoption of Choice Based Credit System
  • Visvesvaraya Technological University: Regulations Governing the Degree of B.E./B.Tech., effective from 2021-22 (clauses 21OB12.2 and 21OB12.3)
  • Anna University: Regulations 2021, B.E./B.Tech. full-time programmes of affiliated institutions, with amendments (clauses 15.1, 15.5.3, 16.2)

Where we have not read an institution's document, we give no formula for it. That is why the list of universities is short. The converter lets you type the formula printed on your own grade card.

What is ours

The explanations, examples and calculators are our own work. The arithmetic of a percentage or an average is the same everywhere; what differs between institutions is the rule, and for that we quote the document.

Limits

Some of the documents are years old, and boards change their schemes. CBSE, in particular, has changed its class 10 assessment more than once; the 9.5 rule belongs to the certificates that show a CGPA. Results from the calculators are a help for your own checking, not an official statement of your result. This site is independent and is not connected with CBSE, CISCE, UGC, VTU, Anna University or any other body.
