Guides to marks, grades and CGPA

What the numbers on a mark sheet mean, how they are worked out, and what the rules of the boards and universities really say.

A calculator gives an answer; it does not tell you whether you asked the right question. Should the CGPA be multiplied by 9.5 or by 10? Is "best of five" something the board awards? Does a high practical mark make up for a low theory mark? These guides answer such questions from the documents of the boards and universities themselves. Where a rule is quoted, the guide names the document, and the documents are linked on the about page.

The guides on basics need no document: a percentage is the same sum everywhere. The guides on CBSE, CISCE and the universities are only as wide as what we have read, and they say so.
